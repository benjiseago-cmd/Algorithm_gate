Launch, don’t chase a health bar.

Damage raises your percentage and makes you fly farther when hit. A ring-out uses one of your five lives. Keep your jumps for a recovery. Platforms can block shots; disappearing platforms stop being cover.

Heavy attacks need a little room.

Normals are quicker. Heavies take longer and may be ranged. Each character’s special does something different. Fight to fill the special bar; use B or / only when it says READY.

Your comeback starts with a net.

While you have lives left, answer a four-choice question about nets of 3D shapes to respawn. Wrong answers and hints cost no lives. Your opponent can move while you think. Your last lost life ends your match.

Player 1 question: W/S or A/D choose, C answer, B hint.
Player 2 question: arrows choose, comma answer, slash hint.
You can also click an answer or the hint button.

Fold the faces in your mind.

A net is an opened-out surface of a solid. Shared edges act like hinges. A cube net has six squares that fold to six different faces without overlap. Opposite faces do not share an edge on the folded cube. A rectangular cuboid in these questions is explicitly not a cube.
