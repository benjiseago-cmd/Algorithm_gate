Stay in fullscreen.

The menu and arena buttons toggle fullscreen for the whole game. You can return to the menu and scroll through fighters and arenas without leaving fullscreen. Full special bars shake; damage becomes larger at 100% and larger again with a pulse at 130%. Reduced-motion settings replace those animations with static highlights.

Choose either solo keyboard layout.

In solo mode, use WASD with C/V/B or the arrows with comma/full stop/slash. Both layouts control your fighter, including comeback questions. In two-player mode, each layout controls its own player. Every new fight starts with a 3–2–1 countdown; movement and attacks begin at FIGHT.

Meet your rivals. Unlock their worlds.

Mr Seago and his classroom are available from the start. Win each solo challenge to unlock your rival and their arena in two-player mode. Your unlocks save in this browser on this device. The computer uses real attacks and returns after a short knockout delay; you answer your own shape questions.

Higher damage. Fewer jumps.

Damage makes you fly farther when hit. You can jump three times below 100% damage, twice from 100% and once from 130%. Landing resets your jump count. A comeback resets damage to zero. Charged specials unleash large offensive attacks; some also help you recover.

Save a special. Change the fight.

Land hits and survive attacks to fill your special bar. When it is ready, B or slash unleashes your fighter’s strongest attack. Look for the wind-up and move out of the danger area. Specials hit hard, but platforms still block their projectiles.

Catch a sky crystal. Become extraordinary.

Glowing crystals fall from the sky during a match. Touch one to become a fire-breathing dragon, a charging war elephant or a very fast gladiator lion for 12 seconds. C / comma and V / full stop become powerful creature attacks. Charged specials still use B / slash. Your transformation ends when its timer runs out or you lose a life.

Five lives. Make every answer count.

A ring-out costs one life. If lives remain, solve a four-choice question about nets of 3D shapes to return. Each wrong answer costs another life and gives you a fresh question. Hints are free. The last lost life ends the match. Your opponent can move while you think. Comeback protection ends when you attack.

Player 1 question: W/S or A/D choose, C answer, B hint.
Player 2 question: arrows choose, comma answer, slash hint.
In solo mode, arrows also choose, comma answers, and slash gives a hint.
You can also click an answer or the hint button.

Find your route.

Moving platforms carry you. Cracked platforms crumble after a short stay, then return. Main spawn landings normally cannot be dropped through. Makeup Box is all crumbling platforms: keep moving as they disappear and return. A respawn landing gets a brief grace period. Platforms block projectiles using their actual shapes. Background lava, waterfalls and scenery are decoration, so the arena stays fair.

Fold the faces in your mind.

A net is an opened-out surface of a solid. Shared edges act like hinges. A cube net has six squares that fold to six different faces without overlap. Opposite faces do not share an edge on the folded cube. A rectangular cuboid in these questions is explicitly not a cube.
