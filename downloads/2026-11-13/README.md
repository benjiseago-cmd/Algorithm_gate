# Algorithm Gate downloads

Latest original algorithm-maths edition — 17 November 2026.

Open **algorithm-gate-updated.html** on GitHub and choose **Download raw file** (the downward arrow above the code). Find the newly saved HTML in your laptop’s Downloads folder and double-click it to play offline. Previously downloaded copies do not update automatically.

For the source ZIP, download **download-backup.html**, open that saved page, and click **Download backup ZIP**. It contains this updated game, the untouched original attachment, the previous revision and testing evidence. The game itself needs only its HTML file.

This revision changes guardian fights:

- Once a hero enters an active fight, both arena edges keep that hero inside. The entrance permits a teammate still outside to join later. Visible seals disappear after victory. Jumping, knockback and surviving falls cannot bypass the boundaries.
- Each fresh correct well answer restores **one heart to each co-op hero**, up to five, including a partner who is still fighting. A reader already at five hearts can answer for an injured partner. Solo restores one heart. Wrong answers and hints remain free, and duplicate submissions cannot award more hearts.
- Ordinary guardian body contact is harmless, including when exposure ends, during armour and while striking. The final descent onto a marked leap landing can still hurt. Projectiles, slam shockwaves, flood, fire, roots, webs, ice and lightning retain their damage and warnings; swinging a branch does not grant immunity to those attacks.
- Guardian knockouts still return heroes to the well with zero hearts. One hero may recover while the other fights. A full-party defeat restores the guardian and clears its attacks without restarting the chapter. Ordinary route-death gate revival is unchanged.

The ten routes, 50 timed beams, puzzles, shared lantern checkpoints, skull/branch animations, algorithm questions and upgrades are preserved. **Slant Gate is unchanged.** The original upload remains separately available as **algorithm-gate-original.html**.

Validation includes 211 arena cases across all ten chapters, 91 shared-well engine checks, 15 well UI checks, 86 contact checks at four frame intervals, 272 slam timing cases, three actual keyboard-binding checks and 169 broader browser/engine checks. Reports and precise limits are in the ZIP. These are automated simulations, scripted Chromium tests and screenshot inspections; no manual full-game playthrough was performed.
